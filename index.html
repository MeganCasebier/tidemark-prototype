Tidemark: payment exceptions

Tidemark is a small-business lender that doesn’t exist. Its payment-exceptions screen has been stuck in review for a month. This is a working prototype of it: click around, then make the decision that unsticks it.

The prototype needs JavaScript to run.

A made-up company with made-up data. Every business, loan and amount is fictional. By Megan Casebier · megancasebier.com
